# Student Course Experiences & Anecdotes

## CS 3110 — Functional Programming & Data Structures

"CS 3110 was the hardest course I took freshman year, but also the most transformative. Learning OCaml forced me to think recursively about everything. The final project (building a functional game engine) was brutal but I came out understanding types and abstractions at a deep level. Office hours are crucial — go every week." — Class of 2025

"Don't underestimate the time commitment. The A2 assignment on red-black trees took me 20 hours. Start early, pair-program when allowed, and read the lecture notes before going to office hours." — Class of 2026

## CS 4780 — Machine Learning

"4780 is math-heavy upfront (probability, linear algebra, calculus). The first two weeks feel like a math class. Once you get to SVMs and neural nets it clicks. The psets are challenging but the TAs are incredibly helpful. Best class I've taken at Cornell." — Class of 2025

"Take it with a friend. Study groups for 4780 are essential. The concepts aren't impossible but you need someone to talk through derivations with." — Anonymous

## CS 2110 — Object-Oriented Programming & Data Structures

"2110 is where you really learn Java and algorithms. The course has a steep learning curve in weeks 4–6 (recursion + trees). After that it smooths out. The labs are well-designed — they actually prepare you for the projects." — Class of 2026

"Prelims are held in the evening. The curve is generous. Focus on understanding Big-O and being able to trace recursive calls on paper." — Class of 2027

## Internship Recruitment Experience

"I got my SWE internship at Amazon through a Cornell-specific referral program. Applied in September, had two coding interviews in October, offer in November. LeetCode medium is the bar — practice graphs and dynamic programming." — CS junior

"Rejected from 40 places before getting an offer. Persistence matters. The ACSU resume workshop in September helped me restructure my projects section entirely." — CS senior
